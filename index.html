CHIP-8 emulator

A 1977 virtual machine, rebuilt from its instruction set. Programs written for it almost fifty years ago run here unchanged.

Programs come from John Earnest's CHIP-8 archive, fetched when you pick one. Each carries the speed its author intended, which is why the slider moves by itself. The eighteen XO-CHIP programs in that archive are left out, since they need instructions this machine does not have. The two test programs are fetched from Timendus' CHIP-8 test suite, which is GPL-3.0 and not redistributed here. They check every instruction against the spec. Games are not included; load your own file.
