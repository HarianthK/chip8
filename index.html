Programs come from John Earnest's CHIP-8 archive, fetched when you pick one. Each carries the speed its author intended, which is why the slider moves by itself. All of them run: the original set, SUPER-CHIP's bigger screen, and XO-CHIP's second colour and its waveform sound. Nothing in the archive records what a program's controls are, so when you pick one, a second machine plays it in the background and the keypad lights up whichever keys it was asked about. The two test programs are fetched from Timendus' CHIP-8 test suite, which is GPL-3.0 and not redistributed here. They check every instruction against the spec. Games are not included; load your own file. The source is on GitHub.
